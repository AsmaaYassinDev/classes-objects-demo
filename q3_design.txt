1. The Identification Questions
What is the Class?
The class is BankAccount. It acts as the blueprint for creating all the accounts.

What are the Objects?
The objects are the specific, individual accounts we create from that blueprint (for example, Sarah's account and John's account).

What Fields does the class need?
It needs AccountNumber (to hold the account's specific number), OwnerName (to hold the owner's name), and Balance (to hold how much money they have).

What Methods does the class need?
It needs Deposit() (to add money), Withdraw() (to subtract money), and GetBalance() (to check the current amount).

What information should the Constructor receive?
The constructor needs the starting information to set up the account: the account number, the owner's name, and the initial balance.

2. The UML Diagram
A UML (Unified Modeling Language) diagram is just a simple box divided into three sections: the class name at the top, the fields in the middle, and the methods/constructor at the bottom.
